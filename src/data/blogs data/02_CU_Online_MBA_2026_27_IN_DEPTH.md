## 2. What Makes the CU Online MBA Different?

The supplied material describes the programme around three major ideas: flexibility, industry exposure and specialisation.

### A management degree without campus relocation

The programme is designed for learners who cannot commit to a conventional classroom MBA. Working professionals can continue employment while studying, while fresh graduates can pursue postgraduate management education without relocating.

### Live and recorded learning

The learning model combines live sessions with recordings. The source states that each semester includes 40+ live sessions, recorded lectures, 20+ case studies and real-world projects, and 80+ Teaching Assistant sessions.

### Dual specialisation

Rather than forcing students into one narrow management area, the source states that CU Online offers a dual-specialisation structure with 23 options. This can allow a learner to combine two areas that support a particular career direction.

### Industry-linked certification content

The source identifies certification content associated with PwC India, PMI and Harvard Business Publishing as part of the programme structure. These claims should be retained only after current programme verification.
