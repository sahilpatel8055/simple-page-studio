## 2. Why Does Specialisation Matter in BBA?

A general BBA introduces learners to management, marketing, finance, HR, operations and business communication. A specialisation goes deeper into one or more areas.

The choice affects:

- Skills you develop.
- Internships you pursue.
- Projects you build.
- Entry-level jobs you target.
- Professional certifications you may add.
- Higher-study direction.

The supplied CU model is notable because it allows students to combine two domains rather than choosing only one.

## 13. How to Choose Your Specialisation

### Step 1: Define the role

Do you want marketing, HR, finance, analytics, entrepreneurship or technology-business work?

### Step 2: Check industry demand

Look for real hiring demand rather than choosing a specialisation only because it sounds modern.

### Step 3: Review the curriculum

Check actual subjects, projects and electives.

### Step 4: Think about higher studies

Consider whether the specialisation supports an MBA, certification or other postgraduate path.

### Step 5: Match your interests

A specialisation takes multiple semesters. Long-term interest matters.
