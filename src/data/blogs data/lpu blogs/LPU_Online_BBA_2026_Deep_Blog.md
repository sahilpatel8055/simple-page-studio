# LPU Online BBA 2026: Complete Course Details, Eligibility, Fees, Syllabus, Specializations & Career Scope

## Introduction

A Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) is a strong starting point for students who want to build a career in management, marketing, finance, human resources, business development, entrepreneurship and related business functions. An online BBA can be particularly useful for students who want the flexibility to study alongside other commitments.

This detailed guide covers LPU Online BBA from the perspective of a prospective student: what the programme is, who can apply, duration, fees, curriculum, learning experience, career opportunities, placement support and important factors to consider before admission.

## About LPU Online BBA

LPU Online offers an online undergraduate learning environment designed to provide students with business fundamentals while allowing them to study remotely. The programme is intended to build an understanding of business operations, management principles, communication, finance, marketing and other areas that support modern organisations.

Students can develop both conceptual knowledge and practical business skills through coursework, assignments, projects, presentations and other learning activities.

## LPU Online BBA Eligibility Criteria

Candidates should satisfy the eligibility conditions prescribed by LPU for the current admission cycle.

Before applying, students should verify the latest eligibility rules because admission requirements can change between academic sessions.

## LPU Online BBA Duration

The Online BBA is a three-year undergraduate programme generally divided into six semesters.

The semester-based structure allows students to progress through foundational business subjects before moving toward more specialised and application-oriented topics.

### Duration at a Glance

- Minimum duration: 3 years
- Total semesters: 6
- Mode: Online
- Study pattern: Semester-based
