# LPU Online BCA 2026: Complete Guide to Fees, Eligibility, Duration, Syllabus, Placements & Career Scope

## Introduction

Technology is now part of almost every industry, creating demand for professionals who understand programming, databases, web technologies, software development and modern computing concepts. For students interested in entering the technology sector, a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) can provide a structured undergraduate foundation.

LPU Online BCA is designed for students who want to study computer applications through an online learning model. This guide covers the programme in depth, including eligibility, duration, fee information, curriculum, learning experience, facilities, placement support, career options and the factors students should evaluate before admission.
