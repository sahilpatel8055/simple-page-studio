## 1. Chandigarh University Online MSc 2026-27: Data Science and Mathematics Complete Guide

Chandigarh University Online's supplied MSc material describes a two-year postgraduate programme with two primary pathways: **MSc Mathematics** and **MSc Data Science**. The programme is positioned for learners who want advanced study in a specialised science domain while retaining the flexibility of online learning.

The two pathways are substantially different. Mathematics is centred on advanced mathematical theory, analysis and specialised mathematical areas. Data Science combines programming, statistics, machine learning, big data and newer AI-oriented electives.
