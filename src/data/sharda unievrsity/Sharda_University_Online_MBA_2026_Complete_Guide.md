# Sharda University Online MBA 2026

Choosing an online MBA is no longer only about studying management from home. For many graduates and working professionals, the decision also depends on the university's academic recognition, specialisation options, fee structure, learning experience, admission process, and career support.

**Sharda University Online MBA** is a two-year postgraduate programme delivered through an online learning environment. The programme is structured across four semesters and combines management fundamentals with specialised learning, projects and industry-oriented subjects.

Sharda University Online offers multiple MBA specialisations, including **Data Science & Analytics, Marketing, Finance, Healthcare & Hospital Administration, Human Resource Management, and Strategic Human Resource Management**. Depending on the selected specialisation, the published programme fee ranges from **₹1,40,000 to ₹1,96,000**.

This guide covers the major details a prospective learner may want to check before applying, including eligibility, syllabus, specialisations, fees, additional charges, EMI options, admission steps, placement support and hiring partners.

# Sharda University Online MBA Specialisations

One of the major features of the programme is the availability of multiple specialisation pathways. The choice of specialisation can influence both the curriculum and the overall programme fee.

## 1. Data Science & Analytics

This specialisation combines management education with data-oriented business decision-making. Subjects include statistical methods, SQL, Python, data visualisation and predictive analytics.

**Suitable for:** Learners interested in analytics, business intelligence, data-driven management and technology-enabled decision-making.

## 2. Marketing

The Marketing specialisation focuses on customer behaviour, sales, distribution, international marketing, service marketing, CRM, advertising and digital marketing.

**Suitable for:** Learners targeting marketing, sales, brand management, customer experience and digital marketing roles.

## 3. Healthcare & Hospital Administration

This pathway applies management concepts to healthcare organisations. It includes hospital planning, healthcare quality, medical records, healthcare project management and hospital information systems.

**Suitable for:** Graduates interested in healthcare administration, hospital operations and healthcare management.

## 4. Human Resource Management

The HR specialisation covers areas such as international HRM, talent acquisition, retention, performance management and compensation.

**Suitable for:** Learners targeting HR, recruitment, talent management, employee relations and organisational development.

## 5. Strategic Human Resource Management

This pathway goes deeper into strategic HR leadership, HR planning, analytics, international HR and change management.

**Suitable for:** Professionals interested in strategic HR roles, leadership and organisational transformation.

## 6. Finance

The Finance specialisation includes fintech, taxation, financial derivatives, risk management, mergers and acquisitions, international financial management and portfolio management.

**Suitable for:** Learners targeting finance, banking, investment, financial analysis and corporate finance careers.
