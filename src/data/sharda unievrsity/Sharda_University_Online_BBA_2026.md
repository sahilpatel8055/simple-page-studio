# About Sharda University Online

Sharda University is located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, and was established in 2009 under an Act of the State Legislature of Uttar Pradesh. Its online education initiative provides learners with access to university-governed digital programmes without requiring them to attend conventional campus classes.

The university is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and holds an NAAC A+ accreditation. Sharda University also promotes an international academic environment, with students from multiple countries and global academic collaborations.

For online learners, the key proposition is the combination of a university-issued qualification, structured academic delivery and the flexibility to access learning remotely.
