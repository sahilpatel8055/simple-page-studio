UGC-DEB Entitlement & Online Degree Validity
Amity University's online programmes are entitled by the University Grants Commission's
Distance Education Bureau (UGC-DEB) to be offered in online mode. Under the UGC (Open and
Distance Learning Programmes and Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020, a degree awarded
in online mode by an entitled university holds the same academic value as the corresponding degree
awarded in conventional (on-campus) mode.

In practical terms, this means the online degree is a bona fide university degree — usable for
employment, competitive/government examinations and further study, subject to the specific
eligibility rules of each employer or exam.

Degree Equivalence — Online Equals On-Campus
A common concern is whether an online degree is 'lesser' than a campus degree. The regulatory
position is clear:

   •   •The UGC's 2020 regulations explicitly establish equivalence between online-mode and on-
       campus degrees from entitled institutions.
   •   •The degree is awarded by Amity University, the same degree-awarding university.

That said, the learning experience differs (self-paced, online), and some employers or foreign
universities may ask for details of the mode. The academic value and eligibility, however, are treated
on par with the regular degree under Indian regulations.

International Recognition & Study Abroad
Amity University is WES-recognised, which supports credential evaluation for higher study and
employment in countries such as Canada and the US.

Recognition of any online degree still depends on the specific university, employer or immigration
authority you're applying to — always confirm their acceptance of online-mode degrees and the
exact credential evaluation they require before applying.
