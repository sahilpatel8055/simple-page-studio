Why is DevOps the Most Hired IT Function?
DevOps is not a role; it is a culture and a skill set that bridges software development and
IT operations. Indian IT is undergoing a structural shift: companies are moving from
waterfall delivery models to continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD)
pipelines. Every major IT services company TCS, Infosys, HCL, Wipro, Accenture and every
product company Amazon, Microsoft, IBM, Razorpay, Freshworks, Zoho now runs
engineering teams structured around DevOps principles.
The demand for entry-level DevOps engineers and cloud operations associates has grown
sharply, yet supply remains limited because most BCA and B.Tech programs still teach
legacy software development without covering CI/CD toolchains, containerization, or
cloud infrastructure management. Parul's DevOps track fills this gap at the undergraduate
level.

verify current course content with Parul directly, as curricula are updated annually:

Parul University's online BBA is delivered through a Learning Management System (LMS)
that provides structured access to all course content, assessments, and student support
resources.
   •   Content Format: Recorded video lectures for all core subjects, supplemented by
       PDF study materials, digital presentations, and case study documents. Live
       session availability for select subjects including doubt-clearing sessions and
       expert industry lectures.
   •   Assignment System: Assignments are submitted digitally through the LMS portal
       with defined deadline windows. Internal assessment marks contribute to the
       semester grade as per Parul University's evaluation framework.
   •   Examination Pattern: Online proctored semester examinations conducted from
       the student's home or office using webcam and stable internet. No travel to an
       exam center is required, eliminating geographic and scheduling constraints for
       working students.
   •   Mobile Accessibility: The LMS supports mobile devices, enabling students to
       access lectures, assignments, and study materials through smartphones critical
