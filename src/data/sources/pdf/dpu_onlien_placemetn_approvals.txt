Factors Influencing Salary Packages of DY Patil Online MBA

An Online MBA offers the flexibility to upskill while you work, but what kind of salary hike can you expect
once you graduate? The truth is, there is not a one-size-fits-all answer. Several factors can influence your
post-MBA salary package.




Let’s understand them one by one.

    Industry

This factor is crucial because it can significantly impact your earnings. You can choose certain industries
that are known for offering high salaries for MBA graduates.

    For example, if you specialise in finance and land a role at a top investment bank in metro cities, you
can expect much higher pay than someone who goes into non-profit management. In addition, you can
go for high-paying sectors such as consulting and asset management, as they tend to offer lucrative
packages for MBAs.

    Online MBA Specialisation

Not all MBA curricula are created equally. Within the programme, your chosen specialisation can play a
big role in earning potential. For instance, choosing an MBA in data analytics may likely open doors to
higher-paying jobs in tech giants compared to a general MBA.

So, if you are searching for a specific industry or role, consider the above factors and choose your MBA
specialisation accordingly.

    Work Experience

Here is where the rubber meets the road. You have completed your MBA, which is impressive, but
employers value real-world experience. The more relevant work experience you have under your belt,
the more your MBA degree is worth.
   For example, if you are an online MBA graduate and have five years of experience in marketing, you
may likely command a higher salary than a recent graduate with no marketing experience, even if you
both have the same MBA degree.

    Pre-MBA Salary

This might seem obvious, but your pre-MBA salary can get a good hike. Generally, the higher your salary
before the MBA, the higher the jump you can expect after.

    Important Fact: MBA graduates who left jobs earning around INR 10 LPA to 15 LPA saw their salaries
increase by 20% -30% after completing online MBA programmes.

    Location

When you search for a new house to buy, you may notice that prices may vary dramatically depending on
the location. Similarly, salaries can vary dramatically depending on job location. Big cities like New Delhi,
Gurgaon, and Bangalore typically offer higher base salaries than smaller towns. Additionally, industry
concentration plays a significant role. Tech giants in metro cities may likely offer more competitive
salaries than tier-2 city companies.

    Negotiation Skills

This might be the most underrated factor, and few people focus on it. Once you have that dream job
offer, you should not be afraid to negotiate your salary. If you have an MBA degree, it shows your
ambition and commitment to professional growth, so use that to your advantage. Furthermore, you
should always research typical salary ranges for your industry and position; this will make a strong case
for your value.
