Yashwantrao Chavan Maharashtra Open University is a state open university established to provide higher education through open and distance learning. Its PG admission system is designed for learners who want to pursue postgraduate study while managing work, personal responsibilities or other commitments.

The YCMOU PG admission model combines structured academic requirements with the flexibility associated with distance learning. Depending on the programme, students may have to complete prescribed coursework, assignments, practical requirements and examinations.

The university's PG options cover different academic objectives. A management graduate may consider MBA, a technology-oriented learner may consider MCA, while students interested in humanities, commerce or science can explore MA, MCom or MSc programmes subject to the programme-specific eligibility conditions.
