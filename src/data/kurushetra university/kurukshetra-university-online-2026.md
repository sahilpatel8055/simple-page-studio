## Kurukshetra University Online: Complete Overview

Kurukshetra University Online is the digital education offering of Kurukshetra University, Kurukshetra, Haryana. Established in 1956, the university is a Category-I institution and carries an **A++ grade from NAAC**. Its Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) offers programmes through both Open & Distance Learning (ODL) and online modes.

For learners who want a recognised university qualification without relocating or attending a conventional campus every day, online education can provide a practical alternative. Kurukshetra University’s online programmes combine instructor-led and self-paced learning, virtual/live sessions, digital study resources, online examinations and academic support.

The university’s January 2026 CDOE admission notice listed online undergraduate programmes such as **B.A., B.Com. and B.B.A.**, and postgraduate programmes including **M.A., M.Com., M.B.A. and M.C.A.** It also listed online diplomas in areas such as Cyber Security, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning and Data Analytics, along with certificate programmes in technology and other areas. Programme availability can vary by admission session, so applicants should verify the current programme list before applying.
