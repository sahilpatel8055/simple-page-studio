## Kurukshetra University Online MBA: Overview

Kurukshetra University Online MBA is a postgraduate management programme
delivered through the university's official online education framework.
The programme spans **2 years and 4 semesters** and combines core
management education with elective-based specialisations.

The curriculum is designed around managerial reasoning, analytical
decision-making, organisational understanding, business research,
functional management and applied learning. Students can access academic
resources through the official KUK Online Learning Management System
(LMS), including recorded lectures, digital study material, assignments,
internal assessments and online examinations.

The programme is particularly relevant for graduates and working
professionals who want to pursue a recognised MBA without relocating or
attending a conventional campus programme.
