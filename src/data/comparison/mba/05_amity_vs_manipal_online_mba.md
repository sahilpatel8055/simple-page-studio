## Scope
This comparison is **Amity Online MBA vs Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) Online MBA**. MAHE and Sikkim Manipal University are separate online MBA programmes and should not be mixed into MUJ's fee or eligibility table.

## Specialisations
Amity's current MBA portfolio includes Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations, International Business, Digital Marketing, Business Analytics, Entrepreneurship and additional specialised variants.

MUJ currently lists 13 electives/specialisations. The wider Online Manipal ecosystem includes Finance, HR, Analytics & Data Science, Marketing, Operations, Supply Chain, Healthcare, Project Management, International Business, BFSI, IT & FinTech, Retail and Digital Marketing.

**Verdict:** Amity has a broad visible MBA portfolio; MUJ offers a more curated structure.

## AI & Digital Learning
Amity's strongest differentiator is its AI-enabled student experience. The university currently promotes **Prof. AMI**, an AI-powered mentor, and the Amigo app for live and recorded learning. It also promotes AI-driven career support.

MUJ provides an online learning ecosystem and the wider Manipal portfolio includes technology-focused areas such as AI, data science and IT/FinTech.

**AI-learning verdict:** Amity has the clearer direct AI-mentor proposition inside the MBA experience.

## FAQs
### Which is better, Amity or Manipal Online MBA?
Amity is stronger for AI/global positioning; MUJ is stronger for a curated programme and MUJ ecosystem.

### Which is cheaper?
MUJ currently publishes ₹1,80,000 standard and ₹1,53,000 after its displayed offer, while Amity publishes ₹1,99,000.

### Which has easier eligibility?
Amity currently publishes 40%; MUJ publishes 50% for general applicants and 45% for reserved categories.

### Is MUJ the same as MAHE Online MBA?
No. MUJ, MAHE and SMU are separate Manipal programmes and should be compared separately.
