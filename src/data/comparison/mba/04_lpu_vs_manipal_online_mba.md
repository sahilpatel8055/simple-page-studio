## Fee Comparison
MUJ currently publishes ₹1,80,000 full fee, ₹90,000 annual and ₹45,000 semester, with a displayed 15% promotional reduction to ₹1,53,000 full / ₹38,250 semester. Application fee is ₹500.

LPU pricing can vary with student grants, programme routes and promotional offers. The comparison page should use the live official quotation for the current intake instead of hard-coding a third-party discounted amount.

**Verdict:** MUJ has the clearer published standard price; LPU may become more competitive after applicable grants.
