## Introduction
LPU and Amity are both strong names in India's online management market, but their propositions are different. LPU combines a large university ecosystem with traditional and emerging management options. Amity Online places greater emphasis on AI-enabled learning, digital tools, global orientation and technology-led career support.

## Specialisations
LPU currently highlights Finance, HR, International Business, Business Analytics, Financial Markets, Supply Chain & Logistics, FinTech & AI, Data Science & AI and Digital/Social Media Marketing among its MBA ecosystem.

Amity lists Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations, International Business, Digital Marketing, Business Analytics, Entrepreneurship and additional specialised MBA variants.

**Verdict:** Both are broad. LPU is particularly attractive for AI/FinTech/analytics pathways; Amity is strong in digital business, global business and entrepreneurship.

## AI & Technology
Amity's clearest differentiator is its AI-powered **Prof. AMI** mentor and Amigo app for live/recorded learning. LPU's differentiation is broader: it offers management programmes focused on AI, data science, fintech and analytics.

**AI support winner:** Amity.
**AI-focused MBA portfolio:** LPU is highly competitive.

## Curriculum & Practical Learning
Amity describes its MBA as combining theory with real-world projects, internships and AI-enabled learning. LPU emphasises industry-oriented curriculum, workshops, guest lectures and practical exposure.

The page should compare actual subjects, project requirements, electives and assessment structure rather than relying on generic claims of "industry relevance".

## Career Support
Amity promotes AI-driven career support and job-placement tools. LPU provides career/placement support through its university ecosystem. Neither should be represented as a guaranteed job provider.
