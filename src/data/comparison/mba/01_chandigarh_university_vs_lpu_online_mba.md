## Which One Should You Choose?
### Choose Chandigarh University if:
- You want maximum specialisation choice.

### Choose LPU if:

## Final Verdict
**CU is stronger for breadth and specialisation variety. LPU is a strong alternative for learners who value the LPU ecosystem and emerging management options.** The final choice should be based on specialisation fit, final payable fee, eligibility and career goal rather than a generic ranking.
