## 14. ROI Analysis

A practical BBA ROI model is:

**ROI = Degree credibility + business knowledge + practical skills + career support + flexibility + affordability**

A higher fee can be justified if the programme offers a clearly better fit for the student's target specialisation or career ecosystem. A cheaper degree may be the better decision when the student is disciplined enough to build internships, certifications and a strong portfolio independently.
