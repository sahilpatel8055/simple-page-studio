# 2. ADMISSION STEPS — VERIFIED

## BAOU — July 2026 ODL

1. Generate ABC ID.
2. Generate DEB ID.
3. Complete GCAS registration for UG/PG.
4. Use the GCAS credentials to apply on the BAOU university admission portal.
5. Complete the BAOU application.

Source: BAOU July 2026 admission notice.

## Shoolini University — Online

1. Register on the official Shoolini Online website.
2. Create/receive the application ID.
3. Fill personal and academic details.
4. Upload required documents, signature and photograph.
5. Pay application fee.
6. Submit application.
7. After confirmation, pay tuition according to the selected payment plan.

## Uttaranchal University Online

1. Open the official online application.
2. Register and verify contact details.
3. Complete personal/academic/professional information.
4. Upload scanned certificates and ID documents.
5. Pay applicable fee online.
6. University reviews the application.
7. Receive admission confirmation.
8. Pay/complete enrolment requirements.
9. Access LMS and begin learning.

## University of Delhi — School of Open Learning

2026–27 UG admissions are direct admission through the SOL/DDCE process; CUET is not mandatory for SOL/DDCE UG admission according to the official prospectus. Candidate must satisfy the programme-specific eligibility.

General workflow:
1. Register/apply online.
2. Select programme.
3. Enter required details.
4. Upload required documents.
5. Pay admission fee.
6. Complete admission confirmation.

## Subharti University CDOE

The current official portal confirms online registration for 2026–27 and a one-time registration fee of ₹1,650 for regular courses. Exact course-level admission workflow should be taken from the current admission portal rather than a generic template.

## KSOU

Official KSOU material confirms online admission activity and online payment. Programme-specific processes can differ. For programmes requiring CET, the documented flow includes:
1. Online registration.
2. Pay CET/application fee where applicable.
3. Download/print application/admission ticket.
4. Appear for CET where required.
5. Check selection list.
6. Attend counselling/document verification where required.
7. Pay prescribed fee after successful verification.
